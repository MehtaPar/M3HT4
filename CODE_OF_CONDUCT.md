# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

M3HT4 welcomes constructive, respectful collaboration from learners and experienced practitioners alike.

Participants should:

- Be respectful and patient
- Share knowledge without gatekeeping
- Keep testing authorized and ethical
- Avoid publishing private or harmful operational details
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementations, not people

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory conduct, or encouragement of unauthorized access is not acceptable.
