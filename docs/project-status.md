## Next concrete milestone

Deliver **one end-to-end M3HT4 scenario** that includes:

1. a sanitized scenario overview;
2. representative security data;
3. a Blue-team hunt path;
4. a Red-team emulation plan;
5. detection and visibility notes;
6. a Purple-team engagement plan;
7. findings and retest templates;
8. a public case study showing lessons learned.

## Why this milestone comes first

Building the full website or a large scenario library before proving the workflow would create unnecessary complexity. The first scenario will test the vocabulary, data model, templates, safety review, and user experience that later features depend on.

## Known constraints

- Development is intentionally incremental and maintained by a small team.
- Public content must not reveal private infrastructure details.
- The framework should remain useful without expensive production infrastructure.
- Internal AI assistance may speed drafting and analysis, but published outputs require human review and should not position M3HT4 as an AI product.
- Third-party tools and content must have licenses compatible with public and potential commercial use.
